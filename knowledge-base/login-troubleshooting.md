# Login and Account Security Troubleshooting

## I cannot log in
Check that Caps Lock is off and that you are using the correct email address. Clear your browser cache or try a private window. If you signed up with Google or GitHub, use that button instead of the password form.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA)
Two-factor authentication is optional but recommended. If you lose your phone, use one of the 10 backup codes shown when you enabled 2FA. If you have no backup codes left, contact support to disable 2FA after identity verification.

## Suspicious activity
If you see a login you do not recognize, change your password immediately and sign out of all sessions under **Settings > Security > Active sessions**. Then open a support ticket with the priority set to high so our security team can review the account.

## Account deletion
You can delete your account under **Settings > Account > Delete account**. Deletion is permanent after a 14 day grace period.
