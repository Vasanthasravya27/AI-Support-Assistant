# How to Reset Your Password

If you forgot your password, you can reset it yourself in under two minutes.

## Steps
1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address linked to your account.
3. Open the reset email and click the link. The link is valid for 30 minutes.
4. Choose a new password with at least 12 characters, including a number and a symbol.

## Troubleshooting
If the reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder and wait up to 10 minutes. Make sure you typed the same email address you used to sign up. After three failed attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes for security reasons.

If you no longer have access to your email address, contact support and we will verify your identity manually. Identity verification can take up to 2 business days.
