# Billing and Refunds

## Billing cycle
Subscriptions are billed at the start of each billing period, monthly or yearly depending on your plan. Invoices are emailed to the account owner and are available under **Settings > Billing**.

## Refund policy
Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement.

Refunds are not available for usage-based add-ons that have already been consumed.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days. Your workspace stays active during this grace period. Update your card under **Settings > Billing > Payment method** to avoid interruption.

## Changing or cancelling a plan
You can upgrade at any time and the price difference is prorated. Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period. Your data is kept for 90 days after cancellation.
